### Note A: the rupiah figure

- 18 May 2026: record close Rp 17.668 (Kontan).
- 20 May 2026, 09.00 WIB (an hour before the speech): all-time weak point Rp 17.748; the day's best Rp 17.605; close Rp 17.654 (Kontan; Bisnis gives the close as 17.629).
- 22 May: close 17.717. 25 May: 17.744. 26 May: 17.796 (record close).
- 29 May (after the upload): 17.843 at 09.04 (RRI), close 17.881 (Kontan).

I found no dated report of exactly 17.845 before 28 May. The pattern he describes (a spike, then back to about 17.600) matches 20 May: 17.748, then 17.605 to 17.654. His point stands either way: the rupiah was already weaker than the 17.500 ceiling when the target was read out.

### Note B: the Netherlands claim

- What Prabowo said (clip c07 and auto-captions at 01:11:26 to 01:12:07 of the official stream): the Netherlands had the world's highest **GDP per capita** in "1500, 1600, 1700, 1800 ... 400 tahun".
- What the presenter says: "PDB Belanda selama 300 tahun tertinggi di dunia". Two differences: 300 against 400 years, and GDP against GDP per capita.
- What Prabowo's own slide shows (on screen in clip c09, titled "Ranking PDB per Kapita Dunia, USD 2011 PPP", source line "Maddison Project Database 2020, University of Groningen; Ranking 2025 dari Pusat Data Bank Dunia, 2026"): 1500: Italia 2.703, Belgia 2.338, **Belanda third** at 2.332. 1600: Belanda first, 4.270. 1700: Belanda first, 3.377. 1800: Belanda first, 4.184. 1929: Amerika Serikat first, Belanda second at 9.068. So the slide supports "first in 1600, 1700 and 1800", not "since 1500" and not 400 years.
- I found no Indonesian fact-check of this claim (searched Tempo, Kompas, Tirto, Detik, Bisnis by keyword). The presenter calls it "fakta juga, faktual" (10:50); the slide only partly bears that out.
- The slide ranks countries; it says nothing about why. That the wealth came from the Indies is Prabowo's reading. I did not capture a historical source on the cause.

### Note C: the moment with the President of the United States

It is the inaugural summit of the Board of Peace at the Donald J. Trump United States Institute of Peace, Washington DC, Thursday 19 February 2026. Before the group photograph, Donald Trump handed the signed Board of Peace document, in its folder, to Prabowo, who was standing behind him, and Prabowo held it up for the cameras. The footage went viral in Indonesia with comments that the president had been made to hold a folder. Clip c12 shows it.
