# Sarajevo, One Wrong Turn (Indonesian)

A 28-second history short narrated in Indonesian about the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand in Sarajevo on 28 June 1914.

It places Sarajevo on the map, introduces the Archduke in the back seat of his car and the six young assassins waiting along his route, then follows the car to the Latin Bridge, where the driver turns the wrong way and stalls a few steps from Gavrilo Princip. It ends on the two shots, the wrong turn that set off a world war.
