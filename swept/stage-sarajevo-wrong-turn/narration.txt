June twenty-eighth, nineteen fourteen. Sarajevo.

One wrong turn is about to start a world war.

In the back seat rides Archduke [Franz](/fɹˈænts/) Ferdinand, heir to Austria-Hungary's throne.

Along his route wait six young assassins.

At the Latin Bridge, the driver turns right. The wrong way.

He brakes, and the engine stalls, a few steps from [Gavrilo](/ɡævɹˈiːləʊ/) [Princip](/pɹˈɪntsɪp/).

He fires twice.
